Hospital Electrical Renovation Project

We successfully completed full electrical renovation of an old hospital building in Shirval, Pune. The goal was to upgrade the entire electrical system to support modern medical equipment and ensure patient safety.

  • Complete rewiring of old electrical system
  • Safe power supply for medical equipment
  • Emergency backup and safety systems
  • Patient rooms and operation area wiring
  • Earthing and protection system installation

Result: Fully safe, modern and reliable hospital electrical system.
