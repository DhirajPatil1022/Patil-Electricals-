About Patil Electricals

Established in Karad, Patil Electricals has been the trusted name in electrical services for over 30 years. Our team of certified electricians handles everything from routine maintenance to complex installations with precision and safety.

We specialize in residential, commercial, and industrial projects, ensuring compliance with all safety regulations. Customer satisfaction is our priority, backed by 1000+ completed projects.

Why choose us? Licensed experts, 24/7 emergency support, and competitive pricing for premium quality work.
